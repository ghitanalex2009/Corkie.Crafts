Crafted with Purpose,
Sourced with Responsibility.

Based in historic St Albans, Hertfordshire, Corkie Crafts was born out of a desire to prevent natural wine corks from entering landfills. Cork oak bark is one of the most eco-friendly materials on Earth—harvested without cutting down a single tree.

We meticulously sort, treat, and design every individual piece to create charming home decor that adds warmth, rich organic texture, and a touch of heritage to your living spaces.
